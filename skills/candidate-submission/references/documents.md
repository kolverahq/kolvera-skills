# Audience-safe recruitment documents

Choose audience and purpose before producing a role pack, candidate report, interview pack or submission summary. Retain an editable source and stable version label.

Candidate-facing role pack: confirmed role/employer where disclosure is allowed, work pattern/location/travel, currency/base/OTE, real work, evidence criteria, interview process and open questions.
Client-facing candidate report: evidence against role criteria, relevant verified experience, availability and approved compensation only. Exclude private screening notes, other candidates, fees/internal margins, recording links and unsupported claims.
Internal scorecard: labelled private; retain contradictions, evidence gaps and calibration separately. Do not attach it to candidate/client delivery.

Use approved assets without hardcoded agency identity. Render every PDF page; inspect clipping, overflow, tables, links and readability. Check text extraction, metadata, comments and hidden/private content. Confirm final PDF matches latest source and intended recipients. If visual review is unavailable, label that limitation and do not claim ready-to-send.

Document generation is performed by the available local/document tools, not assumed to be a finished-PDF Kolvera MCP feature. Creating a file never authorises email/upload/publication. Obtain scope covering destination, recipients and exact version before delivery.
