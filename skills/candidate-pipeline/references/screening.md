# Evidence-based candidate screening

## Before the call
Confirm candidate identity, selected role/desk and purpose. Read authorised profile/history and the actual role brief. Where available, get_candidate_call_prep reads existing preparation; generate_candidate_call_prep creates/refreshes it and may incur cost; inspect current tool before use.
Prepare job-related questions against the same criteria for each candidate. Explain recording/notetaking separately from representation or submission consent.

## During and after
Capture capability examples with context, the candidate's own contribution, scale, outcome and evidence. Distinguish stated from verified credentials and unresolved contradictions. Do not infer work rights, health, protected traits or personal circumstances from names, photos or accents.
Confirm availability, notice, location/timezone, work pattern, compensation currency/period/base/OTE, competing processes, previous applications and excluded employers.
Summarise strengths, gaps, candidate questions and a dated recommendation. Keep internal calibration separate from candidate/client-facing material. Update authorised notes without overwriting earlier history; read back.
Record next action, owner and due date. Interest in a role does not itself authorise named CV submission, public marketing or a recorded call.
